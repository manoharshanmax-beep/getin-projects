About ICSBIGC–2026

The International Conference on Sustainable Business, Innovation & Global Competitiveness (ICSBIGC–2026) is a platform for academicians, researchers, industry experts, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and students to share knowledge, present research, and discuss new ideas in business. The conference focuses on sustainable business, innovation, technology, and global competitiveness. It aims to promote quality research, innovation, collaboration, and sustainable development.
Publication
Selected research papers will be considered for publication in peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings. All accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings with ISBN. Selected high-quality papers may also be published in peer-reviewed journals with ISSN, subject to the review process.

About the Department
The Department of Commerce (Computer Application), established in 1997–1998, is dedicated to delivering quality commerce education integrated with contemporary computer applications. With a student strength of over 300, the department fosters academic excellence through an industry-oriented curriculum, experienced faculty, technology-enabled learning, and outcome-based education. Regular seminars, workshops, guest lectures, industrial visits, and skill enhancement programmes provide students with practical exposure and professional competencies, preparing them for successful careers, higher education, and entrepreneurial pursuits in the evolving global business environment.
